Asset Management

Track and manage physical assets with geographic locations.

Characteristics

  • Create, read, update, delete asset records
  • Detailed asset properties and history
  • Often connected to work orders, inspections
  • Track who changed what and when
  • Find specific assets quickly
  • Different roles (viewer, editor, admin)
  • Accurate, up-to-date information is critical
